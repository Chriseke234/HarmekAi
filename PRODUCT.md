# Harmek AI — Product Context (PRODUCT.md)

## Product Identity
- **Name:** Harmek AI (by ChrisBuilds)
- **Positioning:** "Harmek AI — Your business, always within reach."
- **Category:** Standalone multi-tenant B2B SaaS customer engagement platform.
- **Target Audience:** Non-technical business owners and customer-facing teams across skincare & beauty, e-commerce, retail, laundry & cleaning, salons & wellness, hospitality, and professional services.

## Core Product Truths
1. **Works without an external LLM API:** The Native Harmek Intelligence Engine is the default mode and answers product, price, service, FAQ, delivery, booking, and policy questions directly from structured business records.
2. **Optional Enhanced AI:** Businesses may optionally connect OpenAI, Anthropic, or Gemini with encrypted server-side keys and automatic fallback to the Native Engine.
3. **Human Takeover First:** Any team member can take over a live conversation at any moment; Harmek immediately pauses automated replies until control is explicitly returned.
4. **Honest Metrics & Trial:** 14-day free trial starts on assistant activation (not signup), requires no credit card, never fabricates ROI or revenue claims, and distinguishes Handled, Resolved, Lead Captured, and Sale Completed.

- **Persuade:** Public marketing site (`/`, `/features`, `/how-it-works`, `/industries`, `/pricing`). High editorial contrast, clear value, grounded examples, zero generic SaaS templates.
- **Operate:** Authenticated dashboard (`/dashboard/*`), guided onboarding (`/onboarding`), website chat widget (`/widget/[widgetKey]`), and platform admin (`/admin`). Scanability, clarity, calm density, and instant actionability.
